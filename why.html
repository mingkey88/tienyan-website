PT ESTA Indonesia Partnership

Our partnership with PT ESTA Indonesia combines Singapore's heritage with Indonesia's sourcing and manufacturing expertise to deliver premium edible bird's nest to the global market. By controlling the entire value chain—from upstream swiftlet breeding farms to modern downstream processing facilities—we carefully monitor the nutritional value and quality of every product.

Key Advantages

Global Export Readiness

Officially certified by the CNCA (Registration #002) as one of Indonesia's largest legal exporters of bird's nest to China.

End-to-End Quality Control

Complete traceability and professional quality assurance from farm to final consumer product.

Customized Solutions

The ability to provide high-value, premium bird's nests tailored to innovative and specific consumer expectations.

Quality Control at Every Stage

Every bird's nest goes through a 7-step process designed to support cleanliness, consistency, and product confidence for our business partners.

  1. 01

    Sorting

    Sorts raw bird's nests by shape, feather content, and colour.
  2. 02

    Cleansing

    Rinses the nests to remove dirt and impurities.
  3. 03

    Feather Removal

    Removes feather residues by hand for thorough cleaning.
  4. 04

    Finishing

    Restores the nests to their natural form and presentation.
  5. 05

    Grading

    Grades the nests based on physical features and product requirements.
  6. 06

    Heating

    Steams the nests in a controlled environment to support food safety.
  7. 07

    Packing

    Seals each nest using vacuum packaging for hygiene and handling.
