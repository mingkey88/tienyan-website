Who is Tien Yan

Tien Yan brings together Singapore's bird's nest heritage with Indonesia's premium edible bird's nest expertise.

Our story traces back to Fu An Bird's Nest, a trusted Singapore name established in 1950. Today, that legacy continues through our partnership with PT ESTA Indonesia, combining generations of market experience with integrated sourcing, processing, and manufacturing to serve businesses worldwide.

Our Vision

To be one of the world's most trusted names in premium edible bird's nest.

Our Story

The story of Tien Yan traces its roots to Fu An Bird's Nest, established in 1950 at 19 North Canal Road, Singapore.

For decades, 阜安 Fu An was one of Singapore's respected bird's nest merchants, supplying premium edible bird's nests to renowned restaurants, retailers, and overseas markets. At its peak, Fu An exported to many countries and served well-known establishments including Spring Court, Hillman Restaurant, May Flowers, Hang Kang Restaurant, Ban Seng and many others.

This heritage laid the foundation of deep knowledge, trusted relationships, and an unwavering commitment to quality that continues today.
