Our Story

  1. 1950

    Hoo Ann Bird's Nest (阜安)

    Tien Yan traces its origins to Hoo Ann Bird's Nest (阜安), established in 1950 at 19 North Canal Road, Singapore. For decades, it was one of Singapore's respected bird's nest merchants, supplying premium bird's nests to retailers and renowned restaurants such as Spring Court, Hillman Restaurant, May Flowers, Hang Kang Restaurant and Ban Seng and exporting to markets across the region.

  2. 2026

    Tien Yan Bird's Nest (天燕)

    In 2026, Senior Founding Patriarch Dato Seri Elliot Lim, together with Tien Yan's co-founders, partnered with PT ESTA Indonesia to establish Tien Yan, combining decades of industry expertise with premium Indonesian edible bird's nest sourcing.

Today, Tien Yan supplies authentic, export-ready edible bird's nests to strategic partners worldwide, carrying forward a legacy of quality, trust, and enduring partnerships.

Characteristics of High Quality Bird's Nest.

  • Naturally ivory in colour, with slight variations in shape and texture.

  • Expands two to three times after soaking while retaining its natural strands.

  • Has a light egg-white aroma. House Bird's Nest have a soft and delicate texture while Cave Bird's Nest are crunchy and chewier in texture.
